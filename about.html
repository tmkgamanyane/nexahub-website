About Us

Built in South Africa, for South Africa.

NexaHubApp brings everyday services and the local businesses behind them into one connected platform.

Our story

One login. Every service you actually use.

NexaHubApp was built around a simple idea: the apps people open every day — for a ride, a meal, a place to stay, a flight, a beauty appointment, a rental car, a property search or a quick sale to a neighbour — shouldn't all live in different places. NexaHubApp brings them together into a single account, backed by a growing network of verified local business partners across South Africa.

The platform is operated by NexaHubApp (Pty) Ltd, a South African registered company.

Our approach

Fifteen services, one account, one support line — so switching between them feels like using one app, not fifteen.

For local business

Drivers, restaurants, hosts, agencies, rental operators, beauty providers and companies hiring reach customers and candidates through one Business App instead of building their own.

Trust & safety

Verified business partners, in-app chat instead of shared personal numbers, and clear policies covering every service on the platform.
