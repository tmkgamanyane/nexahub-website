All Services

Everything NexaHubApp does, in one place.

Fifteen modules, one account. Here's exactly what each one is for and who it's built for.

01

NexaChat

Daily chat, plus every service in between

Open to all users

NexaChat is your everyday chat app inside NexaHubApp — message friends and family just like you would on any chat app — and it's also the connective layer across every other service. Message your driver on the way to pickup, confirm details with a host before check-in, or follow up with a beauty specialist about an appointment — all inside one secure, in-app conversation, without swapping personal numbers.

02

NexaRide

E-hailing, on demand

Open to all usersPartners: Vehicle Owners

For Businesses

Vehicle owners register as business partners through the Business App to list their vehicles and manage availability and earnings.

For Users

Clients request a nearby driver, track their trip in real time, and pay in-app.

03

NexaEats

Food ordering & delivery

Open to all usersPartners: Restaurants

For Businesses

Restaurants register as business partners through the Business App, managing their own menu, pricing and order flow from a dedicated partner dashboard.

For Users

Clients order from restaurants near them and track their delivery live.

04

NexaEatsRide

The delivery fleet behind NexaEats

Business partners onlyPartners: Vehicle Owners incl. Motorbikes

NexaEatsRide is the delivery backbone of NexaEats. It's a business-partner-only program: vehicle owners, including motorbike riders, register through the Business App to collect orders from restaurants and deliver them to customers, earning per completed delivery.

05

NexaStay

Accommodation, Airbnb to hotels

Open to all usersPartners: Hosts & Hotels

For Businesses

Hosts and hotel operators register as business partners through the Business App to list availability, pricing and photos.

For Users

Clients search and book accommodation of every kind, from a spare-room Airbnb-style stay to a full hotel booking.

06

NexaFlight

Compare & book flights

Open to all usersPartners: Airlines & Travel Partners

For Businesses

Airlines and travel agency partners register as business partners through the Business App to list routes and fares.

For Users

Clients search and compare flights from different airlines side by side before booking.

07

NexaBeauty

Everything beauty, one platform

Open to all usersPartners: Service Providers & Surgeons

For Businesses

Beauty and aesthetic service providers and surgeons register as business partners through the Business App, listing services, pricing and real photos or reels of past work.

For Users

Clients browse verified providers, view real before-and-after results, and book appointments directly — from nails and hair to slimming and surgical procedures.

08

NexaDrive

Car rental & bus bookings

Open to all usersPartners: Rental Companies & Bus Operators

For Businesses

Rental companies and bus operators register as business partners through the Business App to list their fleet and routes.

For Users

Clients compare rental companies and book a car, or search and book bus tickets from different operators.

09

NexaProperty

Buy or rent property

Open to all usersPartners: Owners & Agencies

For Businesses

Owners and agencies register as business partners through the Business App to list, update and manage their properties.

For Users

Clients search properties to buy or rent, with listings coming directly from owners and agencies.

10

NexaMarket

Buy & sell, person to person

Open to all users

NexaMarket is a pure consumer-to-consumer (C2C) marketplace inside NexaHubApp. List an item, browse what others are selling, and deal directly with other users — no business partner registration needed, since this is user-to-user by design.

11

NexaReels

Short videos, made for scrolling

Open to all users

NexaReels is a short-form video feed inside NexaHubApp. Scroll, watch and share quick videos from other users, all under the same account you already use for every other NexaHubApp service.

12

NexaGlam

High-quality photos & lifestyle content

Open to all users

NexaGlam is where users share high-quality photos and lifestyle content — from everyday moments to standout shots — in a clean, photo-first feed built into your NexaHubApp account.

13

NexaTag

Save images, linked back to their source

Open to all users

NexaTag lets you save and share images from other sources, each one tagged with a link back to where it came from. Other users can click through on any image to reach its original source.

14

NexaGlobal

Global news & breaking updates

Open to all users

NexaGlobal keeps you informed without leaving the app, with a feed covering global news, world updates and breaking stories as they happen.

15

NexaJobs

Companies post jobs, candidates apply

Open to all usersPartners: Companies

For Businesses

Companies register as business partners through the Business App to post job openings, manage applicants, and track vacancies.

For Users

Job seekers browse job listings and apply directly to companies in the app — no separate registration required to apply.

Want to register as a business partner?

Vehicle owners, restaurants, hosts, agencies, rental companies, beauty providers and companies hiring all register through the Business App.
